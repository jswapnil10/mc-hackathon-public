HOW THE LIVE ARENA WORKS

What happens when you start a battle?

One round is one complete stress test. Everything is synthetic: no customer is contacted and no real payment is attempted.

1
Choose the test

Select a fraud family and difficulty. Difficulty changes the safe synthetic behavior—not real attack access.

2
Red builds the scenario

The Red model proposes a bounded plan. A safety gate rejects anything outside the simulator contract.

3
Blue makes event decisions

Blue sees observable payment behavior and legitimate look-alikes, but never sees which events are labelled fraud.

4
Referee reveals the result

After Blue finishes, sealed truth measures what was detected, how much synthetic value was protected, and whether legitimate users were harmed.
